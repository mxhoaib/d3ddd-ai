FAQ

Questions, answered.

What can I upload?

Hand sketches (photos or scans), PDFs, reference images, or a written brief. The more dimensions and notes you include, the more accurate the draft.

Which file formats can I export?

DWG, DXF, PDF and PNG are planned at launch, with layered output so drawings drop straight into your CAD workflow.

Are the drawings ready for construction?

AI drafts are a fast, accurate starting point. Drawings for permits or construction should always be reviewed by a qualified professional. Enterprise plans can include review by D3DDD drafters.

Do you support marble and stone shop drawings?

Yes — slab layouts, piece numbering, cut lists and edge details are a core focus, built on D3DDD’s experience with fit-out and stone fabrication projects.

Is my project data private?

Your uploads and drawings belong to you. Project files are kept private to your account and are not shared with other customers.

When does early access open?

We’re onboarding early-access users in waves. Join the waitlist and we’ll email you when your spot is ready.
